## 中文正文

# JS More

JS More 是 [Jurassic Saga](https://modrinth.com/mod/jurassic-saga) 的附属模组，主要补充原模组缺少的恐龙运输玩法，也让日常养殖和照料更方便。

### 麻醉与运输

用麻醉弩麻醉恐龙，再装进捕获箱，就能把它带回基地，或运到新的地方释放。

途中记得补充麻醉药、水和食物，并留意箱体状况。运输失败时，恐龙会被强制就近释放，在危险地形下仍可能受伤或死亡。

### 养殖与集蛋

JS More 调整了主模组中鸵鸟、鳄鱼等动物的自然消失和繁殖机制，让你也能像养原版动物一样圈养它们。它们不会因为你离开就自然消失，还能通过喂食繁殖，慢慢扩大养殖规模。

在养殖场放上集蛋器，就能自动收起附近掉落的蛋，为吃蛋的恐龙备好食物。接上漏斗，还能把蛋送进储存箱。孵化中的蛋会留在原处，方便继续扩繁。

### 恐龙博士眼镜

戴上眼镜，看向恐龙就能了解它有没有吃饱喝足、身体是否健康，也能查看年龄、性别和产蛋进度。运输途中，还能检查麻醉剩余时间和捕获箱补给。

### 可选的群系开关

主模组群系默认正常生成。若与其他群系模组冲突，可以在配置里按需禁用，作为简单直接的备用办法；重启后生效，已有区块不变。

---

适用于 **Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge**，需要同时安装 **Jurassic Saga** 和 **[Travelers Lib](https://modrinth.com/mod/travelers-lib)**。多人游戏时，客户端和服务器都需要安装。

## English short description

An add-on mod created for Jurassic Saga, introducing a range of additional gameplay features such as tranquilizing, capturing, and transporting creatures. You can also check dinosaur status and raise animals.

## English description

# JS More

JS More is an addon for [Jurassic Saga](https://modrinth.com/mod/jurassic-saga), adding a way to transport dinosaurs and making everyday animal care a little easier.

### Anesthesia and transport

Sedate a dinosaur with the anesthetic crossbow, put it in a capture box, and take it back to your base or move it to a new home.

Keep the box stocked with anesthetic, water, and food, and watch its condition along the way. If transport fails, the dinosaur will be forcibly released nearby. Dangerous terrain can still injure or kill it.

### Raising animals and collecting eggs

JS More changes how Jurassic Saga animals such as ostriches and alligators despawn and breed, so you can keep them much like vanilla farm animals. They won't despawn just because you leave, and feeding them lets you breed more and grow your farm.

Place an Egg Collector nearby to gather dropped eggs for your egg-eating dinosaurs. Connect a hopper to send them into storage. Eggs that are still hatching stay where they are, so you can raise more animals.

### Dino Doctor goggles

Put on the goggles and look at a dinosaur to check whether it's healthy, hungry, or thirsty. You can also see its age, sex, and egg-laying progress. During transport, check how much anesthetic time remains and whether the capture box needs more supplies.

### Optional biome generation control

Jurassic Saga biomes generate normally by default. If they clash with other biome mods, you can disable them in the config as a simple fallback. Restart to apply the change; existing chunks stay as they are.
